Mortgage consultancy

Make your homeownership journey clearer.

Our mortgage consultancy team helps clients understand available housing-finance pathways, prepare documentation and navigate eligibility requirements. Explore the services below and speak with us about your circumstances.

01

25% RSA for Mortgage Equity Contribution

Guidance on the process for eligible Retirement Savings Account holders seeking to use up to 25% of their RSA balance toward mortgage equity contribution, subject to applicable pension rules.

02

Mortgage Application Advisory

Support with understanding lender requirements, reviewing eligibility considerations and preparing the documentation typically needed for a mortgage application.

03

Mortgage Refinancing Guidance

Advisory support for clients exploring refinancing opportunities, including applicable programme requirements, documentation and potential repayment-term changes.
