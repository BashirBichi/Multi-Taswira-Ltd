Before you apply

Eligibility and documents vary by programme.

The list below is a starting point for consultation, not a universal requirement or guarantee of qualification.

Common information to prepare

  • Valid means of identification
  • Employment or income information, where relevant
  • Retirement Savings Account details for RSA-related inquiries
  • Property and mortgage information, where available
  • Existing mortgage statements for refinancing inquiries

Specific documentation, age limits, contribution thresholds and other conditions depend on the current rules of the relevant provider or programme.

Frequently asked questions

Questions clients often ask.

Can I withdraw 25% of my RSA for any purpose?

No. The 25% RSA mortgage equity contribution arrangement is a specific, eligibility-based housing finance pathway; it is not a general-purpose withdrawal.

Does a consultation guarantee mortgage approval?

No. Approval, rates and loan terms are determined by the relevant lender or programme administrator after assessment.

Can you help with an existing mortgage?

We can discuss refinancing-related requirements and help you explore whether relevant options may be available.
